The BIP-39 English wordlist (2048 words), used by src/sync/vendor/wordlist.js.

SHA-256 of the list as english.txt (one word per line, LF endings):
2f5eed53a4727b4bf8880d8f3f199efc90e58503646d9ff8eff3a2ed3b24dbda

The same list, byte for byte, is published in:
  - BIP-39 itself, https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039/english.txt.
    BIP-39 (Marek Palatinus, Pavol Rusnak, Aaron Voisine, Sean Bowe) states:
    "This BIP falls under the MIT License."
  - Its reference implementation, trezor/python-mnemonic
    (src/mnemonic/wordlist/english.txt), under the MIT licence below.
  - The bip39 npm package 3.1.0 (src/wordlists/english.json), where Peek Budget
    took its copy. That package is ISC-licensed: see bip39-npm-ISC.txt.
